### Bugs found & fixed during P1 (worth remembering)

- SHA-256 K[16] constant was corrupted (0xe4936938 → correct
  0xe49b69c1) — caught by the FIPS empty/abc vectors.
- The 432.33 px reference is the mean of the two **fx** values, not all
  four focals (mean-of-4 gives 433.16).
- NIST vector for "abcdbcde..." ends ...06c1 not ...0f31 (typo).
- EEPROM fixed-length records: code A/B records are 4-B payloads;
  build_eeprom must pad codes to record size (serial already padded).
- crossbeam bounded channel: drop-oldest needs a second Receiver clone
  held by the reader (try_recv eviction), else only drop-newest is
  possible.
